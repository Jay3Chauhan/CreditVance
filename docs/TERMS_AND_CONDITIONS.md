## 3. Description of the Service

Credvert is an independent software application designed to help consumers research, compare, organize, and optimize credit cards. Key capabilities provided by the Service include:
- **Card Catalog & Comparison:** An informational database displaying publicly available credit card benefits, annual fees, reward structures, welcome perks, lounge access allowances, and foreign currency markup rates.
- **Reward Engine & Optimization Calculator:** An algorithmic simulation tool estimating potential cashbacks, airline airmiles, and reward point yields based on user-provided spending assumptions.
- **Hardware-Encrypted Secure Vault:** A client-side, zero-knowledge digital organizer enabling users to encrypt and access card details on their local device using biometric authentication.
- **SMS Spend Tracking & Bill Reminders:** An optional on-device parser that reads transactional SMS alerts from registered financial institutions to generate personal spend analytics, track fee-waiver milestones, and notify users of upcoming statement and payment due dates.
