Account and Data Deletion

Application: Credvert (com.jaychauhan.credvert) • Developer: Jay Chauhan • Published: October 18, 2026

Credvert provides complete control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play User Data Policies, Apple App Store Review Guidelines, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Zero-Knowledge Reminder: Your full 16-digit card number, CVV, and expiry date are stored exclusively on your physical phone in hardware-encrypted storage. They are never sent to our servers. Resetting local app data or uninstalling the app permanently destroys all on-device secrets.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Credvert app on your device.
  2. Tap the Account tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account management section.
  4. Tap Delete account (in red).
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete permanently.

Your cloud account and synced cards are purged from our live databases immediately, and your session is logged out.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Category Specific Items Action & Timeline
User Profile Email address, password hash, user ID, display name. Permanently deleted within 24 hours.
Synced Card Metadata Card product name, nickname, last 4 digits, billing/due dates. Permanently deleted within 24 hours.
Spend Analytics & History Parsed spend transactions, merchant names, categories. Permanently deleted within 24 hours.
Push Tokens FCM notification tokens and device links. Revoked immediately.
System Logs (Security) Anonymized request and rate-limiting logs. Overwritten on a rolling 30-day window.
Database Backups Encrypted disaster recovery snapshots. Permanently purged within 30 to 90 days.
