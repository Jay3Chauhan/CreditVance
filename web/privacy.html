Privacy Policy

Application: Credvert (com.jaychauhan.credvert) • Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Developer: Jay Chauhan
Zero-Knowledge Core Promise: Credvert never sends your 16-digit card number (PAN), CVV, or full expiry to any server. Your sensitive card credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and kept solely inside your phone's hardware security enclave (Android Keystore / iOS Secure Enclave). We never sell your data, and on-device SMS parsing never uploads raw SMS texts.

1. Overview and Scope

Credvert ("we", "our", or "us") provides a privacy-first credit card intelligence, rewards optimization, expense tracking, and card management platform. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, local processing, on-device encryption, and your legal rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India DPDP Act 2023), GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA.

3. Information Collected When You Create an Account

If you choose to sign in to sync your cards across devices, we collect and securely sync:

  • Account Information: Email address, hashed password, optional display name, and user ID.
  • Card Metadata (Non-Sensitive): Card product name (e.g. "Infinia"), issuing bank, user nickname, last 4 digits of the card, statement generation day, and payment due date. (Last 4 digits are used strictly for card identification).
  • Spend & Transaction Records: Parsed transaction amount, currency, merchant name, category, transaction date, issuing bank, and last 4 digits for spend insights, fee-waiver milestones, and bill reminders.
  • Shortlist & Preferences: Bookmarked credit cards, comparison trays, and user interface preferences.

4. Financial SMS Processing (Google Play Policy Compliance)

Credvert requests Android READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions strictly under Google Play's Financial Services / SMS-based Money Management exception:

  • Prominent In-App Disclosure: Before any runtime prompt, a full-screen disclosure explains what is read and how it is protected.
  • Affirmative User Consent: SMS tracking is optional. You must tap "I Agree, Allow SMS Access" to enable it.
  • Read-Only Operation: Credvert has no phone or SEND_SMS permissions and cannot send messages.
  • Bank Sender Whitelist: Only SMS messages originating from verified bank sender IDs (e.g., HDFCBK, ICICIT, SBICRD, AXISBK) are processed.
  • Zero Access to Personal SMS or OTPs: All personal chats, non-financial texts, and verification codes/OTPs are filtered out and ignored.
  • 100% Local Parsing: Extraction occurs entirely in on-device memory; raw SMS text never leaves the phone.
  • Instant Revocation: You can pause tracking or erase all spend data at any time under Tracking & Privacy settings.

5. Diagnostics and Technical Telemetry

To keep the app fast and reliable, we use standard Google Firebase infrastructure:

  • Crashlytics: Anonymized stack traces and OS versions upon crashes. No card numbers or CVVs are ever included.
  • Performance Monitoring: Network latency and launch benchmarks.
  • Analytics: Aggregated screen views and button taps (can be toggled off under Account → Settings → Diagnostics).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Device tokens used exclusively to deliver your scheduled bill due reminders.

6. No Sale or Brokerage of Data

Credvert does not sell, lease, or monetize your personal or financial information. We do not share your data with credit bureaus, loan brokers, lead generators, or third-party ad networks. Data is shared only with cloud infrastructure processors (Google Firebase, Supabase/Neon PostgreSQL) bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.

7. Data Retention & Erasure

  • On-Device Data: Cleared immediately when you tap "Reset this device" or uninstall the application.
  • Account Deletion: Initiated via in-app Account → Delete Account or online at credvert.app/deletion. Active user records and synced cards are purged within 24 hours. Disaster recovery backups are permanently overwritten within 30 to 90 days.
